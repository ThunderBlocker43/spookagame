# Credits

## Audio

Every sound in the game is in `public/assets/audio/`, converted to MP3 (and trimmed of
leading/trailing silence for effects) from the original files linked below. All of them
are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free to use, modify and
redistribute, commercially or not, with **no attribution required**. The authors are
credited here anyway, and where a page asked for (optional) credit, that is noted.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
